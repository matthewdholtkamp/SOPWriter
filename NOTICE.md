# SOP Writer Notice

SOP Writer is an independent formatting aid for preparing unsigned local GLWCH publication drafts. It is
not an official DHA system, does not provide records-management advice, and does not apply or validate
CAC-secured digital signatures. Users remain responsible for organizational review, records handling,
signature workflows, and compliance with controlling DHA, Service, and local publication requirements.

letterhead workflows. Official marks are not licensed under the repository's MIT License. Inclusion
